# Credits

Everything in BLOKES is drawn and synthesized in code, except what's listed here. All of it is free to use. Each file's source is listed in `audio/sources.json`.

The sounds were mastered for the game (mono, trimmed, normalized, MP3) and level-matched to its mix by the sound bench (`src/client/dev/soundBench.ts`, from `sound-design/design.py`).

## Sound: CC0 (public domain)

No credit is required for any of these; it's given anyway, with thanks.

### Kenney (www.kenney.nl)

- **Impact Sounds:** metal, plates, punches, soft impacts, concrete steps.
- **RPG Audio:** the axe chop.
- **Casino Audio:** card fans and shuffles (the bills in Make It Rain).
- **Sci-fi Sounds:** force fields, slime and thrusters.
- **Music Jingles:** brass hits and sax licks.
